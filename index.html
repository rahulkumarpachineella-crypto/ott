How the system works

The pipeline, in the order it runs. Nothing here is a stub — every step executes in this page.

Collect behaviour

Ten signals per account: tenure, watch hours this month and last, days since last login, support tickets, failed payments, how much of what they start they finish, devices, plan tier, and whether they were exposed to the last price revision.

Label and split

1,400 historical accounts are labelled churned or retained, standardised to zero mean and unit variance, then split 70/30 so the model is never scored on what it studied.

Train the classifier

Logistic regression trained by full-batch gradient descent with L2 regularisation. Weights stay readable, which is what makes the per-subscriber explanation possible.

Score the active base

Every live account gets a probability and a tier. High is 60% and above, medium 30–60%, low below 30%.

Explain each score

Multiplying each standardised feature by its weight gives that feature's contribution, so the console can name the two or three reasons driving any individual number.

Match an offer

The retention rules read the top driver, not the score. A failed card gets a billing fix, a fading watcher gets a curated row, a price-sensitive account gets an annual discount.

Built as a final-year project. The data is synthetic and generated from a seeded random process, so the numbers are stable between visits but no real person's viewing history is involved.
